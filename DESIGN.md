## Brand & Style

This design system blends the energy of the traditional Argentine neighborhood supermarket with a clean, punchy, and contemporary retail digital presence. It communicates neighborhood warmth, unbeatable promotions, everyday reliability, and honest family commerce.

The visual style is **High-Contrast Commercial Modernism**:
- High-visibility typography paired with an intense crimson red straight from the iconic emblem.
- Clean white and cool-slate surfaces that feel fresh, hygienic, and organized, leaving behind clunky supermarket flyer clutter.
- Tactile, modern promotional badges with high contrast for immediate price legibility.
- Pragmatic, straightforward navigation prioritizing immediate user tasks: checking weekly offers, exploring departments, reaching out via WhatsApp, and finding store locations.

## Colors

The palette establishes an unmistakable hierarchy designed for fast retail browsing:

- **Primary (`#C8102E` / Red):** The authentic brand anchor taken from the circular heritage logo. Reserved for primary conversion buttons ("Agregar", "Pedir por WhatsApp"), deal tags, promotional highlights, urgency countdowns, and active states.
- **Secondary (`#0F172A` / Carbon Slate):** Provides maximum legibility for high-impact titles, product names, prices, and header text. Avoids harsh pure black by keeping a deep mineral undertone.
- **Tertiary (`#25D366` / Fresh WhatsApp Green):** Used strategically for direct messaging integration, customer care actions, fresh produce badges, and stock availability signals.
- **Accent Promo Gold (`#EAB308` / `#F59E0B`):** Supplemental energetic warm yellow for secondary discount tags (`% OFF`, `2x1`), special club savings, and flash deal badges.
- **Surfaces & Neutrals (`#FFFFFF`, `#F8FAFC`, `#F1F5F9`, `#E2E8F0`):** Pure white cards layered over alternating clean slate backdrops to produce crisp separation between shelves and categories without visual fatigue.

## Typography

**Plus Jakarta Sans** provides a sturdy geometric foundation with friendly, rounded counters that echo the full circular curves of the classic red logo. 

- **Display & Headlines:** Use extra bold weights (`800`) with tight letter spacing for supermarket signage, mega-promo titles, and hero value propositions.
- **Pricing:** Treated as distinct display tokens (`price-xl`, `price-sm`) using bold figures to ensure numeric clarity across mobile grids.
- **Labels & Tags:** Uppercase letter-spacing is applied to promotional pills (`OFERTA`, `DESTACADO`, `AHORRO`) to guarantee instant peripheral recognition while scanning products.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured, responsive 12-column layout designed to handle heavy density without visual clutter:

- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12 columns with `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.25rem`. Maximum container width set to `1280px` for optimal scanning. Product grids display 4 columns; category shelves display 6 columns.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, adapting product grids to 3 columns and category feeds to horizontal scroll rails.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with `margin: 1rem` and `gutter: 0.75rem`. Product cards default to 2 columns side-by-side to replicate the tactile feeling of grocery browsing with minimal vertical scrolling friction.
- **Rhythm:** Alternating canvas tones (`#FFFFFF` to `#F8FAFC`) delineate modular functional sections: Hero Flyer, Category Quick-Tiles, Weekly Specials, Instagram Social Feed, Differentials, and Interactive Store Map.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses crisp boundary definitions combined with soft ambient drop shadows to give product packages and discount badges clear lift:

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** Background surfaces (`#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC`) with no shadow.
- **Level 1 (Product Cards & Category Chips):** `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04);` bordered by a hairline stroke `1px solid #E2E8F0`. On hover, elevations transition swiftly (`transform: translateY(-3px)`) with `box-shadow: 0 10px 15px -3px rgba(200, 16, 46, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Sticky Bar & Dropdowns):** `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08);` with a subtle white glass backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px); background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95);`).
- **Level 3 (Modals, WhatsApp Floating Action Button & Toast Alerts):** `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08);`.

## Shapes

The geometric form language builds on the friendly oval contour of the brand's logo:

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Used for input fields, product cards, list items, and standard action buttons.
- **Large Radius (`1rem` / `16px`):** Used for category photo cards, featured flyer containers, map cards, and testimonial/differentiator modules.
- **Full Radius (Pill / `9999px`):** Reserved for promo badges (`OFERTA`, `2x1`), filter chips, quantity steppers, and the floating WhatsApp trigger.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#C8102E`, text white, weight `700`, rounded `0.5rem`, transition all 0.2s. On hover: `#A50D25` with subtle elevation lift.
- **WhatsApp Action:** Background `#25D366`, text white, bold, icon left. On hover: `#1EBE5D`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background, `1.5px solid #0F172A`, text `#0F172A`. On hover: `#0F172A` fill with white text.

### Offer & Product Cards
- Clean white tile with a `1px solid #E2E8F0` border.
- **Top Badge Zone:** Positioned top-left or top-right, featuring an energetic pill badge with `#C8102E` (red) or `#F59E0B` (gold) fill and white uppercase text (`OFERTA SEMANAL`, `-20%`).
- **Media Frame:** Centered high-resolution item imagery against `#F8FAFC` rounded surface.
- **Pricing Block:** Crossed-out regular price in `#64748B`, with the current promotional price in `#C8102E` extra bold (`price-xl`).
- **Cart CTA:** Full-width red button or compact `+` icon button anchored at the card base.

### Category Grids
- Squarish rounded cards (`rounded-lg`) displaying vibrant high-quality department photos (Carnicería, Verdulería, Fiambrería, Bebidas, Almacén).
- Subtle dark bottom gradient overlay (`linear-gradient(to top, rgba(15,23,42,0.85), transparent 60%)`) with high-contrast white bold typography and department highlight tags.

### Sticky Top Bar
- Fixed top navigation with crisp brand logo prominently placed on the left.
- Quick store search input with clear icon and placeholder (`"Buscar ofertas, marcas, productos..."`).
- Quick branch indicator badge with pin icon (`"Sucursales & Horarios"`).
- Direct WhatsApp contact shortcut button with live pulsing status dot.

### Instagram Feed Integration
- Modern 4-to-6 column square tile gallery showcasing daily store stories, fresh meat cuts, bakery arrivals, and community events.
- Hover state reveals an Instagram icon with engagement stats overlay.

### Store Location & Differentials
- **Differentials:** 3-to-4 horizontal micro-cards emphasizing key local benefits: "Estacionamiento Propio", "Envíos a Domicilio", "Atención Personalizada", "Ofertas Todos los Días".
- **Map Section:** Full-width or split 60/40 map block embedded with brand-colored custom map markers, store address, opening hours, and one-tap "Cómo llegar" Google Maps button.
